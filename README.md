One fortune-cookie line for a crypto trader who has just been liquidated. The classic cookie promise — "a great fortune awaits you" — breaks on the truth: the great fortune is the one the trader just lost, and the oracular advice collapses into checking the couch cushions. Fortune voice, trading loss, rueful and warm, with a surprising last word.

> A great fortune awaits you, trader — unfortunately, it is the one you just lost; check the couch cushions.
